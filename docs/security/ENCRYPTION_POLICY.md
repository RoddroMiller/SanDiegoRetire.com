# Encryption Policy — moved

This policy is **firm-level** and is no longer maintained separately in this
repository. Two divergent copies existed (one here, one in `the-one-process`), which
meant an examiner asking "what is your encryption policy?" would get two different
answers.

**Canonical location:** `the-one-process/docs/security/ENCRYPTION_POLICY.md`

Portfolio Architect's system-specific encryption facts — bundled dependencies, the
local `.env` build configuration, what CI does and does not hold, browser storage, and
App Check state — are in **§7.1** of that document.

Consolidated 2026-08-05. See §9 of the canonical policy for what changed, including a
correction to a control this copy described inaccurately.
